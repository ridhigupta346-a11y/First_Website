Our Story & Heritage

Where Tradition Meets Royal Culinary Mastery

Founded with a devotion to authentic ancestral recipes, Spice Garden is a celebration of royal court banquets, rustic earthen tandoors, and hand-milled spices.

Every curry, biryani, and succulent kebab is curated by our renowned Executive Chef and his team of master culinary artisans. We source our saffron directly from Kashmir, our cardamoms from Kerala hills, and our fresh produce from organic local farms.
